# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence, not personalities;
- assume good intent while accepting correction;
- disclose relevant commercial or standards affiliations;
- keep legal claims precise and clearly sourced;
- never publish credentials, private documents, personal data, or vulnerability details;
- respect upstream projects and avoid misleading claims of endorsement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, credential exposure, spam, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate this policy. For sensitive reports, use a private GitHub Security Advisory. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented where safe.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced by a full community-ratified policy as governance expands.
